Pokemon: Inter-generational power creep?

The beloved franchise, Pokemon has had many generations of video games. With each entry Game Freak has an incentive to sell as many games as possible, but how do they encourage more sales? A common theory within the fan base is through "power creep" or the increase in stats as a story or franchise continues. Let's test this theory against the first six generations of Pokemon games.

Generation 1

As you can see by the narrow bands on the graph, over the years the Pokemon franchise kept a rather consistent average stat range. Perhaps instead of increasing the power of each generations to increase sales, as a series famous for allowing intra game transfers, the "magic" of Pokemon is that regardless of which Pokemon Generation is your favorite the series' future games never made your previous games captures feel irrelevant.
